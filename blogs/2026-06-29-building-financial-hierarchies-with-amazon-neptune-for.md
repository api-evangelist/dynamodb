Demonstrates how Amazon's Finance Technology team uses graph database technology to model complex corporate structures, covering an iterative graph modeling approach, Gremlin query patterns for real-time payment routing, and a Primary/State node versioning pattern. The solution addresses multi-hop hierarchy traversals needed for payment routing, cash pooling, and intercompany accounting across hundreds of legal entities spanning multiple jurisdictions.
