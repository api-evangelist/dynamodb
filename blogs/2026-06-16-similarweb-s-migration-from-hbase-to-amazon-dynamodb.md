Managing massive data volumes at scale presents significant operational challenges. At Similarweb we faced these challenges with Apache HBase and found a solution in Amazon DynamoDB. Similarweb is a digital intelligence platform that provides AI-powered insights into website traffic, app usage, and market trends to help businesses benchmark competitors and optimize growth strategies.
